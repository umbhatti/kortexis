Our Mission

Kortexis was founded to eliminate the compute bottleneck in engineering design. By bridging the gap between rigorous structural physics and geometric deep learning, we enable automotive OEMs to iterate in real-time.

We are building the industry's first privacy-preserving foundation model for crash simulation. Our platform allows for unprecedented design space exploration without compromising intellectual property, safety, or engineering accuracy.
