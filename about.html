Founder & Lead Architect

The Kortexis architecture was designed by Usman Bhatti to address the computational latency inherent in legacy solvers.

Leveraging over a decade of domain experience with Jaguar Land Rover's simulation workflows, Usman engineered the Kortexis codebase to bridge the gap between rigorous physics and geometric deep learning.

He now leads the product and engineering teams developing the core solver technology and overseeing the training infrastructure on Google Cloud.
