The Bottleneck

The Latency Trap

High-fidelity solvers like LS-DYNA require massive CPU clusters running for several hours to converge for a single crash event. This limits engineers to simulating <5% of the total design space, leaving innovation potential untapped.

The Solution

Real-Time Inference

We provide a Universal Surrogate Solver that predicts deformation and stress fields in milliseconds. By enforcing conservation laws directly into the loss function, we ensure engineering-grade reliability for safety-critical applications.

Physics-Informed Architecture

Physics-Accurate

Unlike generic AI, our architecture embeds Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) to penalise violations of mass or energy conservation.
