Our Architecture

Kortexis overcomes the limitations of traditional Finite Element Analysis (FEA) by deploying a proprietary physics-informed inference engine.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)

Standard deep learning models often "hallucinate" physics. Our architecture embeds partial differential equations (PDEs) directly into the loss function.

  • Conservation Laws: The model is penalized during training if it violates mass, momentum, or energy conservation principles.
  • Mesh Graph Networks: We utilize geometric deep learning to predict stress fields across complex, non-uniform 3D topologies.

Federated Learning & Security

We address the "Black Box" problem of automotive data silos using a decentralized training approach.

  • Gradient Aggregation: Proprietary CAD data never leaves the OEM's secure infrastructure. Only model weight updates are transmitted to the global solver.
  • Edge Inference: Optimized for low-latency deployment on standard workstation GPUs.
