GeoTransolver Architecture

Kortexis utilises a proprietary GeoTransolver architecture to predict highly non-linear structural crash events directly on unstructured 3D meshes. Designed for seamless enterprise integration, the platform outputs standard D3PLOT files for immediate validation in legacy tools, and integrates natively with NVIDIA Omniverse via OpenUSD to enable real-time, collaborative 3D design iterations.

01. Enterprise Deployment Architecture
